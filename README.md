# **Looper**

The best online audio looper out there
(Disclaimer: Looper is entirely "vibe-coded" because I have no coding skills but really wanted to create a program—so feel free to hate on it :)

## What is Looper?

Looper is a loop station in your browser, inspired by the Boss RC-505 MKII. You record
a loop using your microphone, layer the next part over it, and build a complete beat
in just a few minutes: first the bass, then the hi-hat, then the melody. There is nothing
to install: Looper is a single file that runs in Chrome, Edge, and other modern
browsers—even without an internet connection. Your audio stays on your own device.

## Getting started
1. Open Looper, name your project, and click Start. Grant your browser
permission to use the microphone.

2. Put on headphones, set the tempo, and tap a track. Looper counts in for one bar
and then records exactly one loop.

3. Tap the next track and add the next layer. Use the tutorial in the
settings (⚙) to walk through everything step by step. ## Features
**Recording**
1. 6 tracks, each with independent volume, mute, erase, and record controls
2. Record with a one-bar count-in, or start immediately without waiting
3. Fixed loop length (from 1 beat to 16 bars) or manual stop for short and long loops
4. [Mixed/Overdubbing]
5. Overdubbing: record on top of existing audio instead of replacing it
6. Record into an empty section of a track by combining overdubbing and manual stop
7. Headphone monitoring and microphone level meter
8. Tempo and time signature
9. Tempo from 40 to 240 BPM: adjust via +/- buttons, direct entry, or Tap Tempo
10. Time signatures: 2/4, 3/4, 4/4, 5/4, 6/8, and 7/8
11. Metronome with accent; customizable click sound and volume
12. Tempo is always adjustable: loops follow the tempo change while maintaining pitch (like tape) or remain unchanged; beat-oriented loops keep their transients sharp.

**Smart Alignment**
1. Automatic post-recording alignment: Looper snaps your recording to the beat and learns
2. latency automatically
3. Measure latency using a test tone
4. "Snap to beat" aligns all transients to the grid (1/16, 1/8, or 1/4)
5. Align everything to the first beat

**Editing**
1. Per-track waveform shifting (10 ms or 1/16), reversing, and normalizing
2. Trim handles for dragging edges, with magnetic grid snapping
3. and fine-dragging for extra precision
4. Halve, shorten, and step back per track

**Timeline**
1. All tracks stacked on a single timeline
2. Cut, move, and trim clips, just like in video editing
3. Select multiple clips at once using Shift+click or Shift+marquee selection
4. Set loop length per track (÷2, ×2, or custom)
5. Grab and drag the playhead, pause with the spacebar, zoom in up to 6x

**Audio**
1. Per-track reverb, echo (tempo-synced), and filter
2. Output limiter to prevent distortion

**Projects and export**
1. Start screen showing new and existing projects
2. Save to browser (Ctrl+S) and auto-save
3. Save as a Looper file for sharing or backup
4. Export as MP4 (Chrome and Edge), WAV, or all tracks separately as a ZIP

**Convenience**
1. Undo (Ctrl+Z) and Redo (Ctrl+Y)
2. Keyboard shortcuts: space for pause, 1–6 to record, M for metronome
3. 5 languages: Dutch, English, German, French, and Spanish
4. Dark, light, or automatic theme
5. Icons with tooltips; labels optional
6. Select your own microphone and speakers, with a Adjustable input level
7. A built-in tutorial

**Using it together**
1. Share the `looper.html` file. Everyone opens it in their own browser.
2. Everyone uses their own headphones; otherwise, the microphone will pick up the click track.
3. Settings and projects are stored locally on each device. Share a project via a Looper file.

## Good to know
1. Looper features 6 mono recording tracks.
2. MP4 export works only in Chrome and Edge. In other browsers, choose WAV.
3. MIDI and foot pedal support are not yet available.
4. Drastic tempo changes may affect the sound quality of a loop.
